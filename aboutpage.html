💡 What is the System?

This is an interactive web application designed to post tasks, monitor deadlines, and track student completion rates.

🎯 What is the Purpose?

To ensure that students leave no tasks unfinished and to prevent late submissions of projects and examinations.

🎨 Guide for Colors & Deadlines

  • 🔴 Red: 1 week or less before the deadline.
  • 🟡 Yellow: 3 weeks remaining before the deadline.
  • 🟢 Green: 1 month (30+ days) remaining before the deadline.

📜 Rules

  1. Log in using your official user credentials.
  2. Check your academic requirements daily.
